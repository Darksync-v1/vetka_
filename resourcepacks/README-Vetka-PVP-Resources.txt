Vetka PVP Resources (Minecraft 1.21.1)
======================================

This is an optional vanilla resource pack extracted from 7lBest_pvp_1.

Included:
- Black golden apple item texture
- Dark heart particle texture
- Eat and burp sound replacements

Install:
1. Copy the folder "Vetka-PVP-Resources" to .minecraft/resourcepacks/
2. Enable it in Options -> Resource Packs.

Important:
- This is a separate resource pack, not three independent Vetka ClickGUI modules.
- The source pack is from Minecraft 1.16.5. Its old GUI icons atlas is not directly compatible with 1.21.1's HUD heart sprite system, so this conversion does not claim to replace the HUD health hearts. The dark heart particle texture is included.
- The included textures/sounds are standard resource-pack overrides and are enabled together.
