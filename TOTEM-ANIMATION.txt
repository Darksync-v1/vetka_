TotemAnimation module
=====================

Enable the TotemAnimation module in the Render category. Vetka installs the bundled
Minecraft 1.21.1 resource pack into .minecraft/resourcepacks/Vetka-Totem-Animation,
enables it, and reloads resources. Disable the module to disable the pack.

The resource pack changes the Totem of Undying texture, display transforms, and
item.totem.use sound. The model was adapted from the supplied 1.21.8 pack metadata
to the 1.21.1 resource-pack format.
